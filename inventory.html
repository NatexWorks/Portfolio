THE PROBLEM

Stock records shouldn't
slow a business down.

When products, stock counts, billing, and reporting live in separate places, everyday work can mean repeated entry, missed updates, and slower decisions. This project explores a more connected workflow for small retailers.

03 / WHY CUSTOM?

Not just another
off-the-shelf POS.

A custom web solution can be shaped around the way a shop already works—rather than forcing every shop into the same workflow.

CONSIDERATIONTraditional POS setupCustom web solution
Workflow

Often follows the vendor's fixed flow

Can be tailored to your products and process

Hardware

May require dedicated terminals or add-ons

Can run in a browser on compatible existing devices

Features

Plan-dependent bundles and add-ons

Build the features your business actually needs

Cost structure

Hardware, subscription, support, and add-ons may stack up

Scope and ongoing costs are agreed around the solution

A custom solution is not automatically cheaper or faster for every business. Hardware needs, integrations, support, and the agreed scope determine the real comparison.
